Macro Conviction Sleeve separate account

What this strategy does. Loosely inspired by Stanley Druckenmiller: read the economic backdrop first, then make a few large bets only when it is favourable. Three US Federal Reserve (FRED) series each vote +1 / 0 / −1 on their recent trend: the 10-year minus 2-year yield gap (widening is bullish), high-yield bond spreads (narrowing is bullish) and the Fed's balance sheet (growing means more money in the system, bullish). The votes add up to RISK ON, NEUTRAL or RISK OFF.

It buys only when RISK ON. It has no screener of its own: it takes the core sleeve's buy candidates (best reward-to-risk first), holding at most {{ max_positions }} positions and risking {{ "%g"|format(risk_pct) }}% of the account on each. Exits use the core sleeve's stop, giveback and time-stop rules, and if the reading turns RISK OFF it sells everything to cash. Long-only, never short.

The regime settings are untested starting points; this sleeve has no backtest yet.
